# Universe Bible: TEST-ORGANIC ("Hollow Market")

## Place
**Hollow Market**: a 1940s storybook market town of animals, all brick shopfronts, painted signs and gaslight. Rain more often than not. Palette: warm shop-window amber vs blue-grey street.
**Economy:** everything here is sold with a slogan. The Market Guild owns the sign-painting shop, the newspaper and the radio.
**Power:** the Guild. Every shop pays it to "advise" their signs.
**Recurring locations:** Rennick's Diner · Glint's Emporium · the Square with the big radio · the Crow's attic above the old sign shop.

## Rules
1. Every belief in Hollow Market was sold by someone.
2. The Crow never tells anyone what to do. He only opens a drawer.
3. The drawers hold the real past; anyone who looks can see it.
4. The Crow can't take back a slogan once it's painted.

## Narrator: Old Ink (the Crow)
- **Backstory wound:** for thirty years he was the Guild's best slogan writer. He painted half the signs in town. Then he saw what his words did, quit, and kept every original draft in a cabinet of drawers. Telling the truth is his penance.
- **Reputation:** kids love him (he tells the best stories). Parents find him embarrassing. Sellers hate him; some of them owe him their fortune.
- **Tic:** taps his beak twice on the windowsill before opening a drawer.
- **Silencer:** "Shh. Watch."
- **Ritual close:** "You can keep ___. Or ___. Look again."
