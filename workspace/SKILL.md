# Attention Story Engine

Short animated story videos where a calm narrator exposes a myth and the product shows up as the proof, never the hero.
Every rule here comes from an attention principle (`system/00_psychology/ATTENTION-PRINCIPLES.md`). Reference channels (Quiet Wolf so far) are examples of those principles, not templates to copy.

## Locked sections (read these first)
- 🔒 `SECTION-1-SCRIPT-WRITING.md`: steps 1–7
- 🔒 `SECTION-2-VISUAL-DESIGN.md`: steps 8–9
They override anything else in `system/` if the two ever disagree.

## Before anything
1. Skim `system/00_psychology/ATTENTION-PRINCIPLES.md` (13 principles, 1 page).
2. Read `GOLD-STANDARD.md`. Every script must feel like it.

## Steps
1. **Topic.** A product, or something trending this week (`system/00_concepts/trend-radar.md`).
2. **Myth.** A belief the buyer really holds or worries about (P1 needs a familiar topic). Not trivia.
3. **One fact.** One date + one plain science line, checked online, with its source and exact verb.
4. **Concept.** Show one concept at a time (myth, stakes, reveal, proof test, verdict). Wait for `next` / `approve` / `refine`.
5. **Script.** Gold-standard shape, 3 speakers max.
6. **Principle check + read aloud.** Name the principle each beat activates; cut beats that activate none. If it sounds assembled or like a lecture, rewrite.
7. **Save** to `projects/<NAME>/scripts/CaseNN_<item>_vN.md` (never overwrite).
8. **Visuals (Google Flow).** `system/06_visual/VISUAL-SYSTEM.md` → Generations table + Edit timeline. Example: `examples/organic/Case01_shots_example.md`.
9. **Edit.** `system/07_edit/editing-pacing.md`: pick the edit mode, cut every 2–3s, numbers as title cards, slow down into the verdict.

## The only rules (principle in brackets)
- Open a specific question in the first 10s and close it only at the verdict. [P1]
- The villain reveals himself; the narrator asks, he doesn't accuse. [P4]
- Stakes in line 1, paid off at the end. [P8, P11]
- Flashback = 1 date + 1 science line + 1 image. [P3, P9]
- No sale words until the end; the offer is the lesson's consequence. [P6]
- One accent color, one keyword at a time. [P10, P13]
- No promises about bodies or results; the verdict exposes the seller, it never tells people what to do.
- Never copy a reference's signature lines, characters or look. Use `system/00_psychology/reverse-engineer.md` for new references.
