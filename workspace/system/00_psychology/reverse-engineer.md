# Reverse-Engineer Any Reference Into Principles

Use this for any channel, ad or creator you want to learn from. The goal is never to copy the look or lines, only to extract *why* it holds attention.

## Steps
1. **Measure:** run R5 (`01_research/R5-reference-teardown.md`) and the frame scan (`07_edit/editing-pacing.md` method): top videos, transcripts, cuts per minute, shot lengths, frames.
2. **List techniques:** everything they repeat (a line, a shot, a graphic, a structure).
3. **Map each technique to a principle** (P1–P13). Fill the table:

| Their technique | What it does to the viewer | Principle | Keep the principle as | Their signature (do NOT copy) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Shh. Not another word. Watch this." | Cuts off the argument, promises a payoff | P1, P2 | A short silencer before every flashback | The exact line |
| Wolf narrator on a stage | Instant "truth-teller" read | P3, P7 | A narrator archetype the audience reads in 1s | Wolf, stage, projector |
| "X was never Y" titles | Opens a gap and promises a flip | P1, P4 | A verdict-style title | Their wording |
| Cut every ~2.2s | Repeated orienting responses | P2 | 20–25 cuts per 60s | — |
| Sepia flashback with one orange glow | Isolates the key object | P10 | One accent color in the past | Their palette |
| "Evolve quietly until you're the wolf." | Familiar closing ritual + identity | P12 | Our own ritual line | Their line |

4. **Rebuild with your own signatures:** new narrator, look, silencer, ritual, same principles.
5. **Log** the mapping in `projects/<NAME>/research/R5-<reference>.md`.

## Rule
If you can't name the principle behind a technique, don't adopt it yet. It may be a quirk, not a cause.
