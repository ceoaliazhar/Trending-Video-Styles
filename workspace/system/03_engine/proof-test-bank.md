# Proof-Test Bank

The climax (Act 3): a challenge the villain must answer and can't. Rotate types; never use the same type twice in a row on a channel.

| # | Type | Move | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The missing number** | "Show me the number." Villain has none. | "Show me the roast date." |
| 2 | **The hidden question** | Read the exact question/wording that produced the "fact". | "Read what the 5,000 doctors were asked. Was bacon in it?" |
| 3 | **The paid source** | Reveal who paid for the authority. | "Whose letterhead is that survey on?" |
| 4 | **The before/after** | Show the same thing before the seller arrived. | "The cart, 1937. The cart today. Spot the difference." |
| 5 | **The receipt** | Follow the money to the final number. | "$5,000 at 22%. Pay the minimum. Count the years." |
| 6 | **The name check** | The official-sounding name was invented by the seller. | "Who named it the 'coffee break'?" |
| 7 | **The same-thing test** | Two identical items, different price/label. | "Same bag. Same leather. New price." |
| 8 | **The silent witness** | A character who was there admits it. | Seller: "…I printed it." |

## Rules
- The test must be **visual** (a document, an object, a side-by-side), not just a line.
- The flashback must have planted what the test reveals (setup → payoff).
- Log the type used in `projects/<NAME>/decisions.md` to enforce rotation.
