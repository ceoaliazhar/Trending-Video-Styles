# Conflict Stack

**Why it exists:** our first story draft was a lecture: villain folded instantly, victim risked nothing, narrator won unopposed. (Lesson L3.)

## 5 layers (all required)
| Layer | Device | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Stakes | Victim has something concrete to lose | What exactly is lost if they're wrong? |
| External | Villain argues back with a *tempting* point | Would a real viewer agree with the villain? |
| Counter-attack | Villain attacks the narrator's motive | Does someone say "he's selling too"? |
| Proof test | A challenge that can't be faked (type from `proof-test-bank.md`) | Does the villain have to *show* something and fail? |
| Internal | Victim wants the shortcut | Does the victim nearly choose wrong? |

## Rhythm
Someone says "no" about every 10 seconds. Proof test = climax.

## Disarm move
When the narrator's motive is attacked, he agrees: "He's right. Don't trust me either. Watch." It neutralizes "this is an ad" at the moment the viewer thinks it.
