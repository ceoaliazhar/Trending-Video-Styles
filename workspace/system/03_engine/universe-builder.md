# Universe Builder (replaces "world" with a living place)

A series isn't a set of ads; it's a **town people come back to**. Define it once per channel (Step 0).

## 1. The place
| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Name & look | What is the town called? Era, architecture, weather, palette. |
| Economy | Who sells, who buys, who owes whom? (Myths live in commerce.) |
| Power | Who runs the town? (Guild, mayor, the big seller.) |
| Recurring locations | 3–5 places episodes return to (diner, shop, square, the narrator's perch). |

## 2. The rules (3–5, never broken)
Examples: "Every belief in town was sold by someone." · "The narrator never tells anyone what to do; he only opens a drawer." · "Nobody in town can see the drawer's past except the people who choose to look."

## 3. The narrator's backstory (why HE tells the truth)
A narrator with no past is a lecturer. Give him a **wound tied to the theme**: he once served the sellers, lost something to a myth, or broke a rule.
The town must **know him**: some love him, some are embarrassed by him, sellers hate him.

## 4. Recurring cast (4–6)
Each built with `character-engine.md`. Villains are recurring too; give them reasons the viewer half-likes them.

## 5. Relationship web
Map every pair with `relationship-engine.md`. Each episode pulls on at least one existing thread, so the series accumulates history.

## 6. Visual identity + rituals
Style, palette, caption style · silencer ("Shh. Watch.") · narrator's physical tic before the flashback · ritual close line.

Output: `projects/<NAME>/universe-bible.md` (template in `05_templates/universe-bible-template.md`).
