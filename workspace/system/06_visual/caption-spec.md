# Captions & Edit

## Captions (added in the edit, never generated)
- **Word-by-word (measured on reference):** one word on screen at a time, synced to the voice; keywords larger.
- **Keyword caption:** one bold word per line (a noun from the line: LABEL, BATCH, HEAT, PROOF). Large, white, heavy sans, thin dark outline. Center-lower third.
- **Subtitles:** full line, small, clean, above the keyword or at the top band.
- **Last caption = the offer** ("30% OFF"), held 1.5s.
- **Safe zones (guideline):** keep text out of the bottom ~20% and the right edge (Reels/TikTok UI). Check in the platform preview before export.

## Assembly order
1. Lay shots in script order (Flow Scenebuilder or your editor).
2. Drop dialogue audio per shot; add narrator flashback VO.
3. One music bed, low (licensed / original). Flow ambience under it.
4. Cut on the line: next shot starts on the first word of the next line.
5. Captions → color match → export 9:16.

## Final check
Watch on a phone, sound off: can you follow the story from keywords + subtitles alone?
