# Visual example: Case #1 "Breakfast" (excerpt)

Shows the two tables the skill produces: **A. Generations** (what to make in Google Flow) and **B. Edit timeline** (how to cut it). Script: `Case01_Breakfast_v2.md`. Universe and cast: `universe-bible.md`.

## Look (set once, reused every case)
- **Anchor A (present):** storybook 1940s market town, hand-painted gouache with paper grain, blue-grey rainy street against warm amber shop-window light.
- **Anchor B (flashback):** same world desaturated to sepia, one accent color (cereal-box red), title card for the date.
- **Characters (saved as Flow Characters, names exactly as in the script):** `Crow` (narrator, ink-black, rain on his beak, two taps on the sill), `Mae` (rabbit mother, work apron), `Pip` (rabbit kid, big ears), `Rennick` (fox, diner owner, spatula).
- **Rule:** one speaker per shot; dialogue as `says: "line"`.
