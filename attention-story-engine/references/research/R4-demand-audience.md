# R4: Demand & Audience

**Purpose:** what people actually search/buy, and what they feel (anxieties, desires, identity).

## Procedure
1. Search: "most searched `{CATEGORY}` <year> Google Trends", "who buys `{CATEGORY}` customer segments <year>", "`{CATEGORY}` survey users".
2. Extract: demand ranking (which `{ITEMS}` lead), buyer segments, where they buy, main fear, main desire.
3. Grade sources: news/surveys > vendor blogs. Label vendor data "direction only".
