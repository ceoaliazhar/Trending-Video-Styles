## What we found the first time (Nov 2026)
- POV setup hooks most reliable (Virlo: 32.7% hit high virality vs 19% for how-to hooks).
- Formats winning: POV discovery, "nobody talks about this", "watch this before you buy", UGC.
- Human-sounding hooks beat copy-sounding hooks.
- Black Friday Nov 27 / Cyber Monday Nov 30; creative should move from value → urgency.
