## His 5 acts
0. **Cold open (0–5s):** side character mid-action states a common belief. ("10,000 a day. Everybody knows that.")
1. **Interrogation (5–20s):** "Who told you?" → victim cites authority → one-line reveal → "Watch this."
2. **Time machine:** "Place, Year." datelines, a hard number every 5–8s, escalation ("Then… then…"), mid-video conflict (seller tries to stop him), "then vs now, spot the difference".
3. **Verdict:** flips the opening; the title = verdict line ("The card was never the product. Your debt was."). Question to viewer. Victim's realization.
4. **Ritual close:** binary choice + identity CTA (10/10 videos).

## Addiction levers
Betrayal reveal (status gain) · viewer cast as victim then hero · one open loop held to the end · numbers as proof · plain one-line science · recognition ritual.

## Visual system
1940s storybook animation, amber/sepia, vignette, stage spotlight · cast as moral shorthand (wolf / herd / fat seller) · screen-in-scene flashback · one bold white keyword caption + sentence subs · 3–4 voices in dialogue.

## Funnel
Description → free guide (email capture) + tool affiliate · episodic long-form spinoff.
