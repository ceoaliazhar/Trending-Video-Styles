# Concept Scoring

Score each angle 1–5. Keep the top 5. Show them in rank order, one at a time.

| Criterion | 5 = | 1 = |
|---|---|---|
| **Belief reach** | Almost everyone holds it | Niche belief |
| **Betrayal size** | "I was played my whole life" | Mild trivia |
| **Proof strength** | Dated, sourced, numeric | Vague / single weak source |
| **Visual story** | Clear scene, characters, props | Abstract idea |
| **Conflict potential** | Someone will defend the myth hard | Nobody cares to argue |
| **Verdict punch** | Flip fits a short title ("X was never Y") | Needs explaining |

Ship threshold: **≥ 22/30**. Ties → higher betrayal size wins.

Calibration (our own scoring of two reference videos, Nov 2026; views are real, scores are judgment):
- 1.8M "Supermarket": reach 5, betrayal 5, proof 5, visual 5, conflict 4, punch 4 = 28
- 512K "same bag different price": reach 4, betrayal 4, proof 3, visual 4, conflict 3, punch 4 = 22
