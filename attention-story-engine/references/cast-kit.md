# Cast Kit: per-video characters (no series, no universe)

Every video stands alone. Cast it fresh from the trend, fast.

## Simplicity rule (hard)
- **Max 3 speaking characters:** Narrator · Believer · Seller.
- **1 relationship** (believer ↔ someone they care about, OR narrator ↔ seller). Not both.
- **1 reveal.** One teaser, one payoff.

## Each character = 3 lines, no more
| Role | Want (in this scene) | Trait | Tell (one gesture) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narrator | Make them look again | Calm, dry, never preaches | Brand-fixed (e.g. taps twice before "Shh. Watch.") |
| Believer | Do right by someone they love | Earnest, a bit stubborn | e.g. smooths a child's hair |
| Seller | Close the sale | Charming, believes his own pitch | e.g. flips a spatula when lying |

## Pick the animals by instant read
Narrator = observer (crow, owl, cat) · Seller = trickster/greedy (fox, raccoon, magpie, pig) · Believer = small and earnest (rabbit, hedgehog, mouse, sheep).

## Emotion in 3 beats (not 5)
1. **Show the bond** in the first 5 seconds (an action, not a line).
2. **Face at stake**: the believer is embarrassed or pushes back ("Not now.").
3. **Small repair** at the end: the believer makes their own choice.

## Fixed per brand (set once)
Narrator species + name, silencer ("Shh. Watch."), ritual close line, visual style. Nothing else carries over.
